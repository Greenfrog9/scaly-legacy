    Breeder Advice (Accurate Hobby Practice)

    Starting capital & first animals: A realistic small start is a few hundred to a couple thousand dollars. Normals of common species (corn, ball python, western hognose) are inexpensive; visual morphs and proven heterozygous animals cost more. Do not buy every morph at once — learn one species thoroughly first.
    Choosing snakes beyond the first:
    • Same species only for breeding pairs.
    • Prefer unrelated animals (different breeders / bloodlines) to reduce inbreeding depression.
    • Decide your project goal: produce a specific recessive morph (need two copies), work with incomplete-dominant genes (visuals appear with one copy; “supers” with two), or keep normals for foundation stock.
    • Check sex, feeding response, and overall health before purchase. Captive-bred is strongly preferred over wild-caught.
    • Hognose note: Western hognose are the most common and generally easiest feeders in the pet trade. Eastern hognose can be more challenging (often prefer toads). Southern hognose are less common in the hobby.
    • Ball pythons and corn snakes have the deepest morph libraries; start with simple recessive or single incomplete-dominant pairings before stacking many genes.
    Genetics basics used in this game:
    • Recessive — needs two mutant alleles to look different (e.g., Albino, Axanthic, Anery). One copy = “het” (carrier, looks normal).
    • Incomplete dominant — one copy changes the look; two copies usually create a more extreme “super” form (e.g., Anaconda/Conda and Arctic in western hognose, Pastel in ball pythons).
    • Probabilities shown are classic Mendelian expectations (Punnett square). Actual clutches will vary around those averages.
    Keep vs sell: Keep animals that advance your genetic goals (proven visuals, useful hets, or good foundation stock). Sell extras and animals that do not fit the project to fund the next generation and cover ongoing costs. In real life you also need proper enclosures, heating, and quarantine space for every animal you retain.

    This game simplifies husbandry, incubation timing, and exact market prices for playability while keeping morph inheritance and species choice grounded in real hobby practice.
