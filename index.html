    Gene catalog

    Established alleles for the 12 species in the game. Combo names such as Snow are phenotypes of those genes, not extra loci. Selective lines are listed separately and do not enter the Punnett.

    Advice

    Start small. A pair of normals teaches the loop. Add one gene project after that.
    Loci. Candy and Albino are the same ball-python locus. Motley and Stripe are the same corn locus. Kahl Albino and Sharp Albino are different boa loci, so they can be stacked.
    Inbreeding. Parent-offspring and full siblings are close inbreeds. The game asks before those pairings. Outcross to a shop founder when a line gets tight.
    Problem genes. Spider, Champagne, Woma, and Spotnose can carry a wobble. Super Cinnamon often has jaw defects. Super Jungle often breeds poorly. Those notes are on the animal.
    What is not a gene. Okeetee, green hognose, banana California kingsnake, and locality forms are lines. Paradox is not a color gene.
